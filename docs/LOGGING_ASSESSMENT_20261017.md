## Verification and next decisions

The complete native/source PR150 and PR151 suites passed at their own exact revisions; evidence is in their final PR indices and retained bundle. This assessment inspected the listed implementation/test paths and their documented behavior; it did not run Azure, inject live SDK failures or prove all request logging is safe.

No blanket logger is justified by this review. Keep current quiet CLI and product contracts. Any optional diagnostic logging needs a separate pre-edit contract: safe run/request correlation, timestamp/duration semantics, bounded volumes, error classes, cancellation/partial handling, redaction tests, stderr/stdout separation and retention. Record DC-013 as open design work rather than silently expanding the controls slice or claiming complete observability.
